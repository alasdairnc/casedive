Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

In short

  • What you type is sent to Anthropic to produce your research results. Leave out names and other details that could identify you or anyone else.
  • You don't need an account. If you create one, we keep your bookmarks and search history until you delete them or ask us to delete the account.
  • We don't sell your information and don't run advertising.
  • Several of our service providers are outside Canada, mostly in the United States.

What we collect and why

Scenarios you type

When you run a search, our server sends your scenario to Anthropic, which generates the analysis. To answer repeat requests quickly, we cache the results of a search, case summaries, citation checks and exported PDFs for up to 7 days. An exported PDF includes your scenario. Cache entries are labelled with a one-way code made from the request, so they aren't tied to your name, email or account.

Case citations are checked against CanLII using the citation only. Your scenario is not sent to CanLII.

Case-law reports (optional)

If you flag a case as irrelevant, we store the first 280 characters of your scenario, the case you flagged, the reason you picked, any note you add, and your search filters. We use these reports to improve which cases we show, and delete each one after 90 days.

Accounts (optional)

Sign-in is handled by Supabase. We store your email address, and your password goes straight to Supabase, so we never see it. Sign-in, confirmation and password-reset emails are sent through Resend.

While you're signed in, your bookmarks and your search history (the text of each search, its filters and how many results it returned) are saved to your account so they're available on your other devices. Only your account can read them. When you sign in, bookmarks and searches you made on this device before signing in are added to your account too.

Technical information

  • Your IP address, or your account ID when you're signed in, is used to limit how many requests can be made per hour. These counters expire within about an hour.
  • Our host, Vercel, keeps request logs that include your IP address and browser type for about an hour.
  • When something breaks, an error report goes to Sentry. Scenario text, summaries and sign-in tokens are removed from these reports before they are sent.
  • Vercel Analytics counts page views so we can see overall site usage. It doesn't use advertising cookies.

Stored in your browser

Without an account, your bookmarks are saved in your browser's local storage and your search history stays in the open tab until you reload or close it. If you sign in, Supabase keeps your sign-in session in local storage. When you sign out, your bookmarks and search history are cleared from this browser; they stay in your account. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Information Kept for

Service providers

These companies process information for us, and each one's own privacy policy also applies:

  • Anthropic — generates the analysis of your scenario.
  • Supabase — accounts, bookmarks and search history.
  • Upstash — caches, case-law reports and rate limits.
  • Vercel — hosting, request logs and page-view analytics.
  • Sentry — error reports.
  • Resend — sign-in and password-reset emails.
  • CanLII — citation checks (no scenario text).

Some of these providers store or process information outside Canada, mostly in the United States. While it's there, it can be accessed by courts and authorities under that country's laws.

Your choices and rights

  • Remove bookmarks or clear your search history in the app. When you're signed in, clearing history also removes it from your account.
  • Email us to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account. Deleting an account removes its bookmarks and search history too. We'll reply within 30 days.
  • If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit. Each account can read only its own rows in the database, and service keys stay on our server. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we'll tell you and the Privacy Commissioner.

Children

CaseDive is an educational tool and isn't meant for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy
